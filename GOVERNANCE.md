# Governance

## Current phase: Founder stewardship

a13i is in its founding phase. Acea Spades (Sundragon Tech) acts as steward: final say on content merges, corpus acceptance, and use of the a13i name, exercised in public via pull requests and decision records in `docs/decisions/`.

This is explicitly a bootstrap arrangement. The structures below activate as the project grows.

## Phase 2: Contributor circle

When the project has 10 or more merged outside contributors, recurring contributors gain triage and review rights over the book and corpus. Stewardship of the name and the manifesto remains with the founder.

## Phase 3: Consortium

When 5 or more organizations are actively contributing experiments, funding, or engineering time, a Technical Steering Committee (TSC) forms:

- One seat per member organization, one founder seat.
- The TSC owns the corpus methodology, the experiment review process, and the book roadmap.
- Manifesto changes require TSC supermajority plus founder consent during the first three years.
- Membership requires public commitment to the manifesto, especially section 4 (people first) and section 5 (claims require experiments).

## What the consortium is for

- Maintaining a shared, vendor-neutral corpus of evidence about AI and accessible interfaces.
- Publishing recommended practices that product teams can adopt without negotiating with any single vendor.
- Funding replication of important results.
- Representing the double dividend argument to standards bodies (W3C WAI and others) with data instead of vibes.

## Decision records

Material decisions are recorded in `docs/decisions/` as numbered records: context, decision, status, consequences. Records are immutable once accepted; reversals get new records.

## The name

"a13i" and a13i.org are held by the founder in trust for the project. If the consortium phase activates, name stewardship transfers to a neutral entity designated by the TSC.

## Code of conduct

Contributor Covenant 2.1 applies. In addition: contributions about disabled people's experience must cite or involve disabled people. Nothing about us without us is a governance rule here, with "us" meaning the disability community that this project's discipline comes from.
